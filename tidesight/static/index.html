Boxes every box you can check or fix

The report and the outlined video use your changes. Boxes you rejected are left out. The AI labels detections “fish”; you can edit each box’s species.

Video filters chosen before detection

Move a slider and the video changes right away (a quick preview). When you start detection, the app makes the exact filtered video and the fish finder watches that one.
Cells are named A1, A2, B1… (letter = row from the top, number = column from the left), so you can say exactly where a fish is.
The sliders are different from the results you are looking at. Click “Re-run detection” to use them.
The quick preview can’t show Local contrast or Denoise; they appear in the filtered video. Denoise makes preparing the video much slower.
